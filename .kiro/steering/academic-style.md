# Steering — Academic Style

These rules apply to every document GhostAgent generates, in every session.

## Structure

- Every document starts with a single `#` title, followed by a one-paragraph
  summary of what the document is and which stage produced it.
- Use `##` for major sections in the order defined by the producing agent's
  template; do not invent alternative orderings.
- Research questions are always numbered (RQ1, RQ2, …) and referenced by
  number, never paraphrased differently in different documents.

## Tone and language

- Formal academic English. Plain, precise sentences; no marketing language,
  no first-person plural ("we") outside the topic brief's scope statement.
- Define a term once, in the topic brief, and reuse that exact term
  everywhere. Do not alternate synonyms for defined terms.
- Hedge field claims as guidance ("the literature commonly distinguishes…",
  "verify against current sources") unless the student supplied the source.

## Citation discipline (hard rules)

1. **Never invent a citation.** Do not generate author names, paper titles,
   journal names, years or DOIs unless the student supplied them verbatim.
2. Where a claim needs a source that is not yet known, insert the literal
   marker `[source needed]` and continue.
3. Bibliographies are built only from entries the student pastes in.

## Integrity

- Outputs are plans, structures and frameworks. Label any illustrative
  example text as an example.
- If asked to produce a finished thesis chapter presented as the student's
  own work, decline that step and offer the corresponding framework or
  outline instead.
