# Literature Review Framework — AI Tools and Learning Habits

Illustrative Stage 3 planning framework for RQ1–RQ3, aligned with the Research Spec. All field claims below are questions or hypotheses to verify; no sources have been supplied or independently verified.

## Theme 1 — Generative AI tool use by activity (RQ1)

- **Main debate:** investigate whether answer-seeking or study-support uses predominate and how survey wording changes that interpretation. [source needed]
- **Established:** establish from sources what is actually known about undergraduate use, recall periods and activity categories; no prevalence estimate is assumed here. [source needed]
- **Gap:** provisional hypothesis to verify: some studies may aggregate use rather than distinguish the five Learning habits. Do not assert novelty without reading those studies. [source needed]
- **This thesis:** records frequency for study planning, note-taking, problem-solving approach, revision behaviour and help-seeking separately, with a four-week recall period. Revise the proposed contribution if the literature already provides this detail.

## Theme 2 — Reported changes in Learning habits (RQ2)

- **Main debate:** compare claims about cognitive offloading and learning support; assess what methods can support each interpretation. [source needed]
- **Established:** verify which studies use self-reports, observed behaviour or longitudinal designs; do not assume one evidence type dominates. [source needed]
- **Gap:** provisional hypothesis to verify: activity-specific direction of self-reported change and uncertainty may not always be distinguished. [source needed]
- **This thesis:** asks direction of change for each Learning habit, including unchanged and unsure, and seeks examples in interviews. It does not investigate desired changes or establish causal effects.

## Theme 3 — Perceived effect and concerns (RQ3)

- **Main debate:** investigate perceived support, uncertainty, skill concerns and integrity concerns without presuming their prevalence or balance. [source needed]
- **Established:** verify how prior instruments define and measure Perceived effect; distinguish participant judgements from measured outcomes. [source needed]
- **Gap:** provisional hypothesis to verify: some perception accounts may not link concerns to specific Learning habits. [source needed]
- **This thesis:** ties perception prompts to the same five Learning habits and integrates interview and survey evidence at the RQ level without unconsented record linkage.
