# Research Requirements — AI Tools and Learning Habits

Illustrative Stage 2 Research Spec derived from the scoped topic brief. These are requirements for a proposed study, not empirical findings.

## Requirements

### RQ1 — Use by study activity

WHEN eligible participants complete the questionnaire, THE study SHALL record frequency of Generative AI tool use for study planning, note-taking, problem-solving approach, revision behaviour, and help-seeking separately.

Acceptance: the instrument includes a defined recall period, a non-user option and one frequency item per activity; the results report each item with its denominator and missing responses.

### RQ2 — Self-reported changes

WHEN participants report use of a Generative AI tool, THE study SHALL ask which Learning habits they report changing since first use and the direction of each change.

Acceptance: each activity has increased, decreased, unchanged and unsure options with clear wording; interviews explore examples. Reporting distinguishes self-reported change from observed change and avoids causal claims.

### RQ3 — Perceived effect and concerns

WHEN participants describe their Perceived effect, THE study SHALL collect perceived benefits, concerns and uncertainty linked to the same five Learning habits.

Acceptance: questionnaire and interview prompts map to the activity list; analysis covers positive, negative and mixed accounts, without treating Perceived effect as measured performance.

### Ethics and scope

WHEN any recruitment or collection begins, THE researcher SHALL first obtain the institutionally required ethics determination and use reviewed information and consent materials.

Acceptance: participation is voluntary; contact details for interviews are collected separately from survey responses; no grades or unnecessary direct identifiers are collected; access, retention and withdrawal limits are documented before launch.
